<h1 align="center">eozMP</h1>
<p align="center"><i>eren's music player — customize and enjoy your music.</i></p>

eozMP is a desktop music player for Windows. It organizes your music by artist and album, shows covers and
synced lyrics, and can find and download music through Soulseek.

## Features

- **Your library, tidy:** Artist → Album, with covers, numbers and years; collaborations filed under the first artist
- **Lyrics:** synced lyrics that follow the song (from `.lrc` files, the song's tags or lrclib.net), or add your own
- **Fullscreen player:** big cover and controls on the left, lyrics on the right, over a moving blurred background
- **Mini player:** small always-on-top window with the lyric line, seek bar and volume
- **Playlists, queue panel, favorites, shuffle and repeat** (previous always goes back to the song you heard)
- **Browse** by genre and decade, **search** artists / albums / songs, **listening stats**
- **Soulseek** (through slskd): search, download albums or whole discographies, auto-organize downloads
- **Make it yours:** themes, accent colors (even from the album cover), fonts, lyric styles, visualizer, equalizer
- **Sound (with mpv):** gapless playback, ReplayGain, output device and exclusive mode
- Media keys, system tray, drag and drop, backup and restore

### New in 1.4

- **Steadier:** a mistake in one feature no longer closes the player (it is written to `.simple_music_player_crash.log`)
- **Space** plays and pauses again after you click around; it only presses a button you reached with Tab
- **Duplicate finder** is safer: the same file seen through two paths isn't a copy, copies keep their folders,
  playlists and favorites follow the copy that stays
- **TV and phone:** a TV that refuses a song gives the sound back to the PC, the TV gets volume and seeking,
  the remote isn't left open after casting
- **Backup restore** keeps this PC's own program and network settings
- Smaller memory use, readable links on the light theme, long wishlists searched in turns

### New in 1.3

- **12 languages:** the whole app in English, Türkçe, Español, Deutsch, Français, Português, Русский, العربية, 中文,
  हिन्दी, 日本語 and Bahasa Indonesia (and lyrics translated into them, line by line)
- **One-click installer** that asks step by step, includes slskd and sets up your Soulseek login for you
- **Phone remote:** scan a QR code and control eozMP from your phone, or listen on the phone itself
- **Play on a TV or speaker** (DLNA / UPnP: smart TVs, Sonos...)
- **Windows media controls:** song and cover in the volume flyout and on the lock screen, headset buttons
- **Last.fm** scrobbling and similar artists
- **Fix tags and cover** from MusicBrainz, recognising songs by their sound (AcoustID)
- **Audio quality check** finds fake 320k / FLAC files; **find a better copy** on Soulseek replaces them
- **Missing songs** of an album, **duplicate finder**, **moods** (Calm / Energetic / Night)
- Soulseek **wishlist**, **trusted users**, **new releases** from your artists
- Fluid fullscreen background, and *repeat this song* now restarts the song on **Next**

## Download

Get **`eozMP-Setup-1.4.exe`** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and double-click it. Setup asks
step by step what to install (eozMP, Soulseek support, VLC for the equalizer, a desktop shortcut) and, if you want
Soulseek, your Soulseek name and password, then sets everything up. No Python needed.
Windows may show a blue *"Windows protected your PC"* box because the app is new and unsigned:
click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Build it yourself

1. Install [Python](https://www.python.org/downloads/) and tick **Add Python to PATH** in the installer.
2. Download this repository (green **Code** button → **Download ZIP**) and extract it.
3. Double-click **`build_exe.bat`**. It installs everything and puts `eozMP.exe` on your Desktop.
4. Optional: with [Inno Setup 6](https://jrsoftware.org/isdl.php) installed, **`build_setup.bat`** builds the
   installer too (it downloads slskd and fpcalc once).

Translations live in `eozmp_lang.py`, generated by `tools/make_translations.py` (hand fixes go in
`tools/overrides.json`).

Or run it straight from the source:

```
pip install -r requirements.txt
python musicplayer.py
```

### Privacy policy

eozMP does not collect anything about you and has no server of its own. It connects only to the services below,
and only for the feature named next to each one:

- **GitHub** (api.github.com): once a day, to see whether a new eozMP version is out. Turn it off in
  *Settings → "Tell me when a new version of eozMP is out"*.
- **lrclib.net**: lyrics of the song you play.
- **MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, iTunes Search**: covers, album and artist details.
- **AcoustID**: only when you use *Fix tags* to recognise a song by its sound.
- **Last.fm**: only after you log in to Last.fm in the settings (scrobbling, similar artists).
- **Google Translate / MyMemory**: only after you pick a language under *Translate* in the lyrics (choose *Off* to stop).
- **Soulseek** (through slskd on your own PC): only when you install Soulseek support and search or download.

Settings, playlists and your library stay on your PC.
